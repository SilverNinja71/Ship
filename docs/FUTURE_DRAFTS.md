## 3. Ship-Reactive Character Outfits & Poses

When the player switches ships in the **Shipwright**, the Captain Minion and crew automatically adapt their outfits and poses to match the vessel's vibe:

1. **Woolly Wanderer (Default Starter — Cream & Mint)**
   - **Outfit**: Classic pastel banana overalls (`#FFF3A8` / `#CFE2F7`) with a tiny paper-fold sailor cap.
   - **Pose**: Cheerfully steering the wooden helm on deck, or leaning on elbows behind the slanted cream desk.
2. **Golden Mane (Honey Buttercream)**
   - **Outfit**: Fluffy pastel lion-mane hood (`#FCE49C`) with soft apricot scarf (`#FAB8A5`).
   - **Pose**: Gently swinging on the lawn swing when idle, or waving a tiny sun-shaped bookmark during focus.
3. **Floating Galley (Sky Milk & Peach)**
   - **Outfit**: Puffy cream baker's toque (`#FFFDF7`) + pastel sky-blue apron (`#BCE2F2`).
   - **Pose**: Stirring a steaming pastel soup pot near the galley chimney on deck; at the study desk, sipping warm tea beside a stack of recipe-style study cards.
4. **Sunfish (Lemon Chiffon Submarine)**
   - **Outfit**: Cozy pastel diving goggles (`#C9F0FA` lenses) + lemon-yellow rain slicker (`#FCEB9D`).
   - **Pose**: Peeking through the round submarine portholes on deck, with tiny floating bubbles rising above their head during deep-focus sessions.
5. **Crimson Corsair (Strawberry Milk Galleon)**
   - **Outfit**: Strawberry-rose captain tricorne (`#F4B6BE`) with a butter-gold ribbon (`#FCE3A1`), zero scary skulls—replaced by a cute pastel heart-and-crossbones doodle.
   - **Pose**: Looking proudly through a tiny brass spyglass at the bow, or stamping completed Pomodoro tasks with a wax-seal stamper at the desk.
